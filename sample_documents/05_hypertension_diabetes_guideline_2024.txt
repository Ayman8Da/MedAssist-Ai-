TITLE: Blood Pressure Management in Adults with Type 2 Diabetes (Fictitious Guideline, 2024 Edition)
SOURCE LABEL: MedAssist Synthetic Library
VERSION: 2024.2
PUBLICATION DATE: 2024-09-15
CATEGORY: cardiology

1. SCOPE

This fictitious guideline replaces the 2019 edition and covers adults aged 18
years and older with type 2 diabetes and hypertension, including adults above
75 years, adults with frailty, and adults with chronic kidney disease.

2. RECOMMENDED TARGET

Treatment should be individualised rather than applied uniformly.

- Adults aged 18 to 64 years without frailty: target clinic systolic blood
  pressure below 130 mmHg, if tolerated.
- Adults aged 65 to 79 years: target systolic blood pressure 130 to 139 mmHg,
  accepting that lower values may be appropriate in selected patients.
- Adults aged 80 years and older, or with frailty or orthostatic hypotension:
  target systolic blood pressure 130 to 149 mmHg, prioritising avoidance of
  hypotension, falls, and acute kidney injury.

The 2024 edition explicitly withdraws the single universal target of below
130/80 mmHg used in the 2019 edition.

3. FIRST-LINE THERAPY

For most adults, any of an ACE inhibitor, an angiotensin receptor blocker, a
thiazide-like diuretic, or a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker may be
used as initial therapy. Choice should reflect comorbidity, cost, adherence,
and patient preference. An ACE inhibitor or ARB remains preferred when albuminuria
is present.

Combination therapy with two agents from different classes is recommended when
blood pressure is more than 20/10 mmHg above target at presentation.

4. MONITORING AND DE-ESCALATION

- Check renal function and potassium within one to two weeks of starting or
  increasing an ACE inhibitor, ARB, or diuretic.
- Assess orthostatic blood pressure in adults aged 65 and older.
- Review the need for continued treatment after any fall, syncope, or episode
  of acute kidney injury.
- Deprescribing should be considered when the burden of treatment exceeds the
  expected benefit.

5. EVIDENCE BASE AS STATED IN THIS FICTITIOUS DOCUMENT

The 2024 edition claims to incorporate newer trials that enrolled older adults
and adults with frailty, and argues that intensive lowering produced smaller
absolute benefit and more adverse events in these groups. This claim is part of
the fictional document and is not a verified statement about real trials.

6. COMPARISON WITH THE 2019 EDITION

- Target population expanded to include adults above 75 years, frailty, and
  chronic kidney disease.
- Blood pressure target changed from a single universal threshold to an
  age-stratified and frailty-sensitive range.
- First-line therapy changed from ACE inhibitor or ARB for everyone to a choice
  among four classes, with ACE inhibitor or ARB preferred when albuminuria is
  present.
- Added explicit de-escalation and orthostatic hypotension guidance.

7. INTERPRETATION NOTE

The two editions differ in the recommended target and in the recommended
first-line agent. Whether this constitutes a genuine contradiction or a
legitimate update depends on the evidence each edition considered and on the
population each addresses. A reader should check publication dates, target
populations, and the underlying evidence before concluding that the documents
conflict. Where a newer edition explicitly supersedes an older one, the newer
edition should generally be preferred, while noting the older evidence base.
